Wayfile

A tidy, private file manager for Android, with Wi-Fi transfer to your computer and NAS.

What it does

  • Browse, search and organize every file, with tags, a file basket and a trash you can restore from.
  • Free up space: find large files, duplicates and app caches.
  • Send files to your computer over Wi-Fi from any browser, protected by a one-time PIN.
  • Use your NAS or a shared PC folder: browse, back up your phone, and keep folders in sync.
  • Open and extract ZIP files, and add people from contact cards (.vcf) to your contacts.

Private by design

  • No account or sign-up.
  • Your files stay on your phone. Transfers go straight to your own computer or NAS on your local network.
  • Wayfile never reads your contacts; it only hands a contact card to your phone's Contacts app when you ask.

Details

  • Android 8.0 or newer, phones and tablets.
  • 14 languages, including right-to-left Arabic.
  • Free with small ads; a one-time Pro purchase removes them.
