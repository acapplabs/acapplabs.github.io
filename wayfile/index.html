Wayfile

A tidy, private file manager for Android, with Wi-Fi transfer to your computer and NAS.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

Everything your files need,
in one clear app

Browse and organize

Every file and folder, with tags, a file basket and a recycle bin you can restore from.

Find inside documents

Search the text of PDF, Word and Excel files, right on the phone.

Free up space

Find large files, duplicates and app caches, and see what fills your phone.

Computer over Wi-Fi

Send files to any browser on your computer, protected by a one-time PIN.

NAS and shared folders

Browse your NAS or a shared PC folder, back up your phone and keep folders in sync.

Archives and contact cards

Open and extract ZIP files, and add people from .vcf contact cards to your contacts.

Private by design

Your files stay on your phone

  • No account or sign-up.
  • Transfers go straight to your own computer or NAS on your local network.
  • Every computer connection is approved on the phone, with a PIN that works once.
  • Wayfile never reads your contacts; it only hands a contact card to your phone's Contacts app when you ask.
