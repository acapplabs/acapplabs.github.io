Wayfile

A tidy, private file manager for Android, with Wi-Fi transfer to your computer and NAS.

Coming soon to Google Play
A closer look at WayfileTap a number to see what it does

What it does

Everything your files need, in one clear app

Tap a card to see the details.

Browse and organizeEvery file and folder, the way a computer shows them.
  • Two panes side by side for copying and moving.
  • Undo after copy, move, rename and delete.
  • Tags, a file basket for collecting files, and a recycle bin you can restore from.
  • Go straight to any folder by typing its address.
Find anythingSearch names, and the text inside documents.
  • Search the text of PDF, Word and Excel files, right on the phone.
  • Categories for images, videos, audio, documents, archives and apps.
  • "Added today" lists everything that arrived today.
Free up spaceSee what fills your phone and clear it.
  • Large files, duplicates and app caches.
  • The camera copy is kept as the original when duplicates look alike.
  • Shrink photos and videos before sharing them.
Computer over Wi-FiSend files both ways from any browser.
  • Nothing to install on the computer.
  • A one-time PIN and approval on the phone.
  • Download a whole folder as one ZIP; live progress with speed and time left.
NAS and shared foldersWork with your network storage.
  • Browse, upload and download on SMB network locations.
  • Back up your phone's photos, documents and messaging media.
  • Keep a folder in sync with one-way sync; transfers resume after a dropped connection.
Archives and contact cardsZIP files and .vcf cards open inside the app.
  • Open, extract and create ZIP files, including old Windows ZIPs with Turkish names.
  • See the people in a .vcf file and add one or several to your contacts.

Every feature, step by step

Install, start-up time, file operations, cleanup, PC and NAS transfer, languages and permissions, each with an expected result.

Attacks tried on purpose

Wrong PINs, requests from another computer, crafted ZIP files and misleading file names are all refused.

Real-world conditions

Locked screens, dropped Wi-Fi, open networks, battery savers and very large files.

Usage tips

Get more out of Wayfile

Small things that make a big difference.

Not sure what a tile does?Every home screen tile has an ⓘ button.
  • Tap ⓘ to read what the tile is for and how to use it, with a shortcut to open it.
Send files to your computerSame Wi-Fi, a browser and a PIN.
  • Open PC transfer in Wayfile.
  • Type the address it shows into the computer's browser.
  • Enter the PIN and approve on the phone.
On a shared computer?Tick "This is a shared computer" when signing in.
  • The session ends after 2 idle minutes and when the tab closes.
  • Delete what you downloaded before you leave.
Changed your mind?Undo is right there.
  • After copying, moving, renaming or deleting, tap Undo in the address bar.
  • Deleted items go to the recycle bin first, so you can restore them.
Transfers stop in the background?Some phones pause apps to save battery.
  • Settings has a battery guide with the exact steps for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, OPPO, vivo and others.
NAS only at homeKeep network locations to Wi-Fi.
  • Each network location can be set to connect only over Wi-Fi, so it never uses mobile data.

Private by design

Your files stay on your phone

  • No account or sign-up.
  • Transfers go straight to your own computer or NAS on your local network.
  • Every computer connection is approved on the phone, with a PIN that works once.
  • Wayfile never reads your contacts; it only hands a contact card to your phone's Contacts app when you ask.
