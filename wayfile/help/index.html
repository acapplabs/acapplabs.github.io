Help

Answers to common questions about Wayfile.

Common questions

How do I send files to my computer?

Connect the phone and the computer to the same Wi-Fi. In Wayfile, open PC transfer, type the address it shows into the computer's browser, enter the PIN and approve the connection on the phone. Nothing needs to be installed on the computer.

The PC page does not open.

Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi; connections over mobile data are refused on purpose. Type the address exactly as Wayfile shows it, including the number after the colon. Some guest and public networks block devices from seeing each other; try your home network.

Where do files from my computer go?

To Download/Wayfile/Computer by default. The PC transfer screen shows the folder and lets you pick another one.

Transfers stop when the screen is off.

Some phones pause apps to save battery. Wayfile's settings have a battery guide with the steps for Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, OPPO, vivo and others.

How do I get a deleted file back?

Deleted items go to the recycle bin first. Open the recycle bin from the side menu and restore the item. Right after deleting, you can also tap Undo.

Does Wayfile upload my files anywhere?

No. Files only leave your phone when you send them: to your own computer, your network storage or an app you choose. See the privacy policy.

How do I remove the ads?

A one-time Pro purchase inside the app removes them.

Not sure what a feature does?

Tap the ⓘ button on a home screen tile. More tips are on the Wayfile page.

Still need help?

Write to info@acapplabs.com with your phone model, Android version and Wayfile's version (Settings > About).
