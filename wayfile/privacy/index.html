Wayfile

Privacy policy

Türkçe

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how your data is handled when you use Wayfile, the Android app made by AC App Labs ("we"), an independent developer.

1. In short

  • Wayfile needs no account, sign-up or login.
  • Your files stay on your phone. We do not collect, see or send your files, contacts or personal information to a server of ours (we do not run one).
  • Files only leave your phone when you send them, and only to the device you choose (your own computer or network storage).
  • The free version shows Google AdMob ads; for ads, Google collects some device data (section 5).

2. Data processed on your device

File operations (viewing, searching, moving, copying, deleting, compressing, opening ZIP files, cleanup analysis) happen entirely on your phone. To work, the app stores on your phone:

  • Settings (language, sorting, preferences).
  • Network locations: the computer/NAS addresses you add, with their user names and passwords. Passwords are stored encrypted with Android's hardware-backed key store (Android Keystore) and cannot be decrypted on another device.
  • Transfer history, tags, the trash and the search index.

This data does not leave your phone. Only settings are included in Android's own backup; network locations and passwords are not backed up. Uninstalling the app, or Settings > Apps > Wayfile > Storage > "Clear data", deletes all of it.

3. Internet and local network use

File management works without the internet. The app uses a network only in these cases:

  • PC transfer: the phone connects directly to the browser of your computer on the same Wi-Fi network; files never pass through the internet or a server. The connection is protected by a one-time PIN and approval on the phone, and does not work over mobile data. Because the connection is unencrypted (HTTP) on the local network, the app warns you on open (password-free) Wi-Fi networks; we recommend not using it on such networks.
  • Network locations (SMB): connects only to the computer or NAS you added.
  • Ads, purchases and updates: through Google services (section 5).

Wayfile does not read your contacts and does not use your location, camera or microphone. When you add someone from a contact card (.vcf) file, the card is handed to your phone's Contacts app and you confirm the save.

5. Third-party services (Google)

Google AdMob (ads): ads are shown so the app can be offered for free. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Interactions with ads, device and app information, diagnostics

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, no ads are shown before you give your choice in Google's consent form; you can change it later in the privacy options in Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads. Pro shows no ads.

Google Play Billing: the Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; only the purchase confirmation reaches the app.

Google Play in-app updates: the app checks for new versions through Google Play.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Children

Wayfile is a general-audience tool; it is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Because we keep no personal data about you, we have no records to delete, correct or export. The data on your phone is under your control (section 2). You can exercise your rights regarding ad data (for example under GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) with Google, and change your ad choices as described in section 5. You can write to us with any questions.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date changes. We also announce important changes in the app.
