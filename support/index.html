Wayfile: common questions

How do I send files to my computer?
Connect the phone and the computer to the same Wi-Fi. In Wayfile, open PC transfer, type the address it shows into the computer's browser and enter the PIN. Nothing needs to be installed on the computer.

Does Wayfile upload my files anywhere?
No. Files only move when you send them, and only to your own computer or NAS on your local network.

Not sure what a feature does?
Tap the ⓘ button on a home screen tile to see what it does and how to use it.
