Common questions

About AC App Labs

How do I contact you?

Write to info@acapplabs.com, or use the contact page to start an email with the right subject. You can write in English or Turkish.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with the app's name and version, your phone or computer model, its system version, and what you did just before the problem. A screenshot helps a lot.

Do your apps collect my data?

Each app has its own privacy policy that says exactly what happens. Wayfile keeps your files on your phone; its free version shows Google ads, and Google collects some device data for them. See Wayfile's privacy policy.

I found a security problem. What should I do?

Please tell us privately first: see how to report a security issue.

Which languages do you support?

Wayfile is available in 14 languages. Our website is in English, with Turkish versions of the legal pages.
