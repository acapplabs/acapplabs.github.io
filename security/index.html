Security

Privacy is where we start

What we do to keep your files and data safe, in plain words. Tap a card to read the details.

No account, no server of oursYour files stay on your phone.
  • Wayfile works without sign-up or login.
  • We do not run a server that receives your files.
  • Files leave the phone only when you send them: to your computer, your network storage or an app you choose.
Protected computer transfersYour computer has to prove it is allowed in.
  • A six-digit PIN that works once.
  • Every connection is approved on the phone.
  • The session is tied to that computer and ends when the phone locks.
  • Too many wrong PINs stop the server.
  • Connections over mobile data are refused.
Careful on open networksA warning before you use an open Wi-Fi.
  • On Wi-Fi without a password, Wayfile warns before starting a transfer and keeps a warning on screen while it runs (Android 12 and later).
  • Outgoing internet traffic is always encrypted (HTTPS).
Safe on shared computersInternet cafés, schools and libraries.
  • Mark the computer as shared when signing in: the session ends after 2 idle minutes and as soon as the tab closes.
  • The page signs out and clears itself after a while without activity.
  • After disconnecting, it reminds you to delete downloaded files.
Encrypted passwordsNetwork passwords can't be read off the phone.
  • Saved NAS and PC passwords are encrypted with Android's hardware-backed key store and can't be decrypted on another device.
  • They are left out of backups and device transfers.
  • Editing a network location never shows the saved password.
Safe with untrusted filesArchives and file names can't trick the app.
  • ZIP entries that try to write outside the target folder are skipped, and free space is checked first.
  • File names with hidden direction characters can't pass an app off as a document.
  • Other apps can't make Wayfile open or copy its private data.
Tested like an attackerThree rounds of review before launch.
  • A code review for weaknesses, a deep review of every file operation, and a hands-on attempt to break the app from the same Wi-Fi.
  • Every finding is fixed and re-tested before release.
Fast security updatesFixes reach you quickly.
  • Security fixes are released through Google Play.
  • Important updates are offered as soon as you open the app and must be installed to continue.
  • Every change is listed in a public changelog.

Found a problem?

Report a security issue

If you think you have found a security problem in one of our apps, please tell us privately first.

Write to info@acapplabs.com with "Security report" in the subject, what you found and how to reproduce it. Please give us time to fix it before sharing it publicly. The full details of what Wayfile does with data are in the privacy policy.
