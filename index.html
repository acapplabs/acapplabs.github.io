The tough tests

We try to break it before you can

Our security approach

Attacked on purpose

About 30 attack attempts from the same Wi-Fi: wrong PINs, other computers, forged requests. All refused.

Booby-trapped files

ZIP files that lie about their size or try to escape their folder, and a 20,000 × 20,000 pixel image. No crash, nothing written outside.

Tricky file names

Names with hidden direction characters that disguise an app as a document are shown as they really are.

Real-world networks

Open Wi-Fi without a password, mobile data, and a phone locked in the middle of a transfer.

Many phones, many versions

Two real phones, and Android 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 17 on emulators, from a 79-step test list.

Speed, measured

Cold start timed on a real phone: about a third of a second on a recent Xiaomi.
