The studio

Who we are

An independent software studio

AC App Labs builds apps for phones and computers that are clear to use and respectful of your data.

We are small on purpose. Nobody else decides what goes into our apps, so we can keep them simple, private and well tested. Wayfile, our first app, started as the file manager we wanted on our own phones.

Our goals

What we aim for

Software people trust

Apps that do what they say, keep your data on your device and never surprise you.

Clear for everyone

Plain words and readable screens in many languages, for experts and first-time users alike.

Built to last

Careful releases, a public changelog and fixes before new features.

Privacy by default

No account where none is needed, and nothing leaves the device unless you send it.

Apps and games

Listening to users

Our journey

How it started

  1. 01October 2026

    Wayfile begins

    A file manager in the style of a desktop explorer: two panes, copy and move with Undo, recycle bin and tags.

  2. 02October 2026

    Phone meets computer

    Transfer to a computer's browser with a one-time PIN, and to NAS and shared folders over the local network.

  3. 03October 2026

    14 languages and this website

    Every screen translated, including right-to-left Arabic; acapplabs.com goes live.

  4. 04Now

    Testing and security review

    Real-device tests and three rounds of security review before the Google Play release.

  5. 05Next

    Google Play, Steam and more

    Wayfile's release on Google Play, our first PC game coming to Steam, and a new Android app now in design and testing.
